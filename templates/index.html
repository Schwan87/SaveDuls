Is FastDownloader completely free to use?
Yes, 100% free with no limits. You never need to register an account or pay any subscription — now or in the future.
Can I extract audio only as an MP3?
Absolutely. Once you paste a supported URL, the result card automatically provides a high-quality 320 kbps MP3 alongside all available MP4 resolutions.
Where do the downloaded files get saved?
Files are sent directly through your browser's standard download mechanism — they land in your browser's default download folder (usually ~/Downloads).
Is it safe? Does it store my data?
We store zero personal data, URLs, or download history. No cookies, no analytics trackers, no third-party data sharing. Your privacy is fully respected.
My video link isn't working. What should I do?
Ensure the link is publicly accessible (not private or age-restricted without login). Copy the full URL from the browser address bar. If the issue persists, the platform may have recently changed their API — try again in a few minutes.
